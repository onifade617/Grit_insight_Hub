Lafiya AI

A multilingual, voice-first community health platform

Lafiya helps families, health workers and government partners act on health information in the language people actually speak. Families can ask questions by web chat or WhatsApp voice note in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin or English, powered by N-ATLaS, Nigeria's own large language model.
